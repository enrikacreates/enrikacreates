A digital diary and planner where a task is never rewritten, only promoted. One day at a time, scored for play and reflection as well as output.

Enrika Greathouse · product design and build · used daily since 2026

The problem

Every planner makes you copy the list again

A task you did not finish today gets rewritten tomorrow, and the week after, until you stop trusting the list and start keeping it in your head.

Rewriting is the tax

The same line, re-entered, loses its history and its age every time.

Someday and today are one list

With no stages, a three-month ambition sits beside buy milk and both get ignored.

Only output counts

Rest, play and reflection are invisible, so a good day can score as a failure.

The belief it is built on

You never rewrite the task. You promote it.

One line, written once, keeps its identity all the way from someday to the twenty-five minutes you are spending on it right now.

The palette

Warm paper, one teal, five soft signals

A planner is looked at all day, so the ground is paper rather than screen. Colour is reserved for state: what is live, what is done, what kind of effort a thing was.

Design throughline

Nothing asks you to go and look

Most planners collect good data and then bury it behind a tab nobody opens. Everything here speaks up where you already are.

Coaching on the page

The momentum bar sits at the top of today and changes its line as the day fills.

The score opens in place

Tap the points and the breakdown appears over the day you are looking at.

One mechanic, four views

Today, Schedule, Week and Board are the same work at different ranges.

Rest counts

Play, nourish and reflect score alongside work, so a Sabbath is not a zero.

The one surface you travel to is Stats, and it is for quarters and years. That split is deliberate, not an accident of navigation.

The day

One turn, with vision at the centre

Vision sits in the hub because the app says so itself: the north star that everything else flows from. It decides what earns promotion.

Two tracks run through the middle of the day and are kept apart on purpose. Actions complete and travel the funnel. Daily Do's recur and reset.

Both score into the same seven categories, which is what lets one number mean something across a whole day.

The funnel

Two homes, one row of commitment

Setup

The data the system genuinely needs

A lot to ask before anyone has done anything, and here it is earned: you cannot promote toward a vision you have not stated.

This is the clearest case I have built of setup being load-bearing rather than a chore screen wearing a welcome hat.

Home Base

Today, and the one thing you are on

The NOW slot holds a single task with a running timer. Everything else on the day is queued behind it.

VIPs are ranked one to three. The Dailies sit above as a tabbed card: story, vision, and the recurring do's.

Coaching, in place

Six tiers, sized against the day you planned

Ready, Spark, Momentum, In the flow, Almost peak, Peak flow. Nineteen lines in the pool, picked by tier and by whether a focus session is running.

The funnel, in the product

Every action and project, across each stage

The recurring track

Routines and rituals, never on the board

Things that complete travel the funnel. Things that recur reset. Most planners conflate the two, which is why a list holding both finish the tax return and drink water serves neither.

Write

Free write, then make a line into an action

A coaching toggle, prompts you can shuffle or write yourself, speak-to-write, and video, recording, embed and image attachments.

The AI coaches you on your own words rather than generating for you. Select a line from the freewrite and promote it into a real action for the day.

The feedback step

The score opens on the day you are already on

Seven categories: move, play, nourish, clean, be, create and work. Rest and reflection score, so a deliberate day off is not a zero.

This is the step that gets used. It never asks you to navigate anywhere, which is exactly why.

The seasonal layer

Stats is for quarters, not for Tuesday

Read at the end of a quarter or a year, against the season of life you were in, rather than as a daily scoreboard.

I built this and I hardly open it. That is the design working: everything with a daily cadence was deliberately put in the path instead.

Vision

The north star everything else flows from

Who I am, a one-line direction, and a three-year picture. The app's own words for this page are the hub of the loop diagram.

It is the reference frame that decides what deserves promoting, which is why it is a mode rather than a settings screen.
