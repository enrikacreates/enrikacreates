Story Leads

the why behind every choice

The story is the PROBLEM the hero is trying to solve. They're the hero, we're the trusted guide, creating a clear path to the shared goal. Everything else is in service to this, all the way to the end, where the hero is changed and equipped for the next journey they choose.

Story =

The experience is intentionally designed to guide them to where they (already) want to go, with personality, candor and emotional resonance.

their mountain, not ours

For Me first (then let it ripple)

Build for my own real problem first. I gotta love it, then share it with fam, then tribe, then friends of friends and my people worldwide. Who I design for and how it rolls out, in widening rings, like a rock dropped in water.

The impact starts narrow and strong, then widens, until it's too wide to be effective and fades.

when it fades, drop the next rock

Meet them where they are

Happy (calm, at home). Sad (out of control, trust broken). Angry (frustrated). Delighted. Design around their current state.

Seen. Heard. Celebrated.

Also challenged, connected, supported. Help people feel known: their name, last week's note, a birthday.

my matrix, from coaching for Seth

Heart, Hand, Head Flow

Map every flow on three levels, milestone by milestone: the emotional journey, the actions they take, the thoughts they're thinking.

Magical Moments Map

Intention on the highs and the lows. People remember the peaks and the ending, so design for both.

Show over tell, dialogue over monologue

Humans lean in to the visual, the interactive, the sensory. That's how connection and resonance happen.

No Dead Ends

First run, empty, loading, error, offline, back after a month.

feeling lives here too

Humans Welcome Here

Messy, vulnerable, imperfect, fun. Personality over blandness. Your full expressed self (and if you're genuinely buttoned up, own it), soaked into the pixels we push. Magnify the personality behind the org.

Make It Real

go the distance

Prototype in hand

make it real, not just feel real

Come with a show-and-tell prototype, not a pitch. Show a screen, don't just talk about it. Get it to hands-on: "I can use it."

Real people using it with real emotion, beautifully shot (beautiful is a value), over generated stock.

a real prototype: screenprinted totes for my book bundle, gifted at a workshop

for video and photos: video over images, human over perfect

Notice and note

ABC: always be curious. Notice and note in the wild. Then zoom out and recognize the patterns that lead to insights.

ears up, heart open

Context-aware crushing

Capture what makes you feel something. It builds taste, keeps you current without copy and paste, sharpens intuition.

Build for what they do, not what they say

Live web prototypes behind an invite-only door. Real users in their own world, captured third-party style. Watch them miss the button you thought was obvious. Ship before it's ready.

why I built betterstories

Put it out? Did it float?

A share, a mention, a testimonial, quietly showing up time and time again. Use is the strongest signal.

watch for the light in their eyes
